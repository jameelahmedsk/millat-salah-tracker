Install Millat Salah Tracker App

Millat Salah Tracker App works offline and saves your prayers automatically! Add it to your phone's home screen in 2 taps:

🤖 Android (Chrome)

Tap the ⋮ (3 dots) at the top-right of Chrome, tap "Add to Home screen" or "Install app", and confirm adding Millat Salah Tracker App.

🍎 iPhone / iPad (Safari)

Tap the Share button (□↑) at the bottom of Safari, scroll down, and tap "Add to Home Screen" to save Millat Salah Tracker App.
